## What is new in 0.1.4

- **Updates find you sooner:** Atelier looks for an update every hour, and each time you come back to it. When an update is ready and Atelier is in the background, macOS tells you. Press the notification to read what is new.
- **What's new after the restart:** after an update, a small "What's new" button shows in the title bar. It opens the changelog once. Close removes it.
- **The update sheet:** the picture now fills the sheet with round corners, and the icons are easier to see.
