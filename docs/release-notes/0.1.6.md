## What is new in 0.1.6

### Improved

- **The changelog:** each note shows its kind in colour: Added, Improved, Faster, Fixed, Changed or Design. A hairline parts the notes, and they scroll when the window is short.
- **The version:** it moves to the bottom left of the status bar.
- **The star:** it is soft gold on dark themes and full gold on light ones.

### Fixed

- **Zoom:** the title bar and the foot of the window keep their gaps when the interface is zoomed.
