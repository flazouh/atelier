## What is new in 0.1.10
Released: 2026-10-09
- **Usage:** the usage view is now a full page. The sources are at the left and the details of the one you pick are at the right. Press the chart icon on the rail, or a limit in the status bar.
- **Usage:** the days of a session show as small bar charts, and the range switch uses the same tabs as the rest of the app.
- **Tabs:** the moving mark under the chosen tab stays in place when you zoom the app.
