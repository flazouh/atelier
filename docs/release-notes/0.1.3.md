## What is new in 0.1.3


### Fixed

- **Claude is found again:** Atelier now reads your shell's PATH when it starts. After an update, or when you open it from the Finder or the Dock, it finds `claude` and your other tools. The "claude is not installed on this host" error is gone.
- **Settings:** dragging the mouse on the Settings page no longer opens the reply box of the conversation behind it, and no longer moves the focus.
